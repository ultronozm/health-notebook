#+TITLE: Nutrition targets

Optional targets supplied by you or your clinician. Leave unset until chosen.
Metric: Cal, Carb, Fat, Protein, or Sat. Direction: range, minimum, maximum,
or track. Use Lower/Upper for a range, Target for a minimum/maximum.
Units: kcal for Cal, g for the other metrics. Notes can record source/date.
